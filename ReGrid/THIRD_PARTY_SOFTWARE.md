ReGrid depends on third-party Python packages that remain subject to their own
licenses and terms of use. In particular, ReGrid uses **OpenSeesPy** for
structural analysis.

OpenSeesPy is **not redistributed as part of ReGrid**; users install it
separately as a dependency. According to the official OpenSeesPy repository
and documentation, OpenSeesPy is free for research, education, and internal
use. Commercial redistribution of applications or cloud-based services that
use `import openseespy` may require a separate commercial license.

Users are responsible for checking and complying with the current terms of
OpenSeesPy and all other third-party dependencies before redistributing or
commercializing software based on ReGrid.
